Tidelight: Reef Rescue – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Tidelight: Reef Rescue ("Tidelight", "the game") is made by RedCurtain ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. If you have any questions, contact us at info@redcurtain.com.au.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account, and we don't collect personal information ourselves.
  • Your progress, coins and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show and measure ads.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

Tidelight saves your level progress, stars, best times, coins, swaps and quakes, settings (such as music and sound) and whether you've bought "Remove ads" on your device only. It isn't sent to us. Deleting the app (or clearing the browser's site data for the web version) deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android and iOS versions show full-screen ads between levels, provided by Google AdMob. To choose, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address (used to estimate your general location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can still play if you decline; you'll see non-personalised ads.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking / Apple Advertising).
  • Buying "Remove ads" stops ads from showing in the game.

Purchases

Coin packs and "Remove ads" are sold through Google Play or the Apple App Store, under their terms and privacy policies. They process your payment; the game only receives confirmation of what was bought so it can give it to you, and can restore "Remove ads" if you reinstall.

Information we collect

We (RedCurtain) don't collect, store or sell any personal information from the game. If you email us, we'll use your email only to reply to you.

Children

Tidelight is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
